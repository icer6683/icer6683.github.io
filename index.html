Andrew Lou

My research asks how AI changes access to expertise and the institutions that produce and apply it. I am also especially interested in the science of AI evaluation and AI's impact on the law.

Previously, I was project lead for Legora's U.S. evaluations team and a summer associate at Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz.

I am a JD student and Chae Fellow at Yale Law, where I work with Professor Daniel Markovits and Professor Daron Acemoglu. I hold a MS in computer science from UPenn and a BS in finance and statistics from Wharton, summa cum laude.

Please feel free to reach out at andrewlou135@gmail.com! If you are on the fence about getting in touch, consider yourself encouraged to do so :)
